Demo stock food photography from Unsplash. Not restaurant photographs.
License: https://unsplash.com/license
Source photo IDs and URLs are recorded in src/data/restaurantData.ts.

The responsive `thali-concept-*.webp` hero variants are AI concept imagery
and are labeled in the interface.

V4 concept imagery and material textures are listed in docs/v4-food-assets.md.

The WebP portraits in `mascot/` are original expression artwork generated
using the supplied character reference. Five expression portraits share the
same framing, with a sixth portrait for blinking. The small eye and pupil
layers come from the idle portrait and drive the desktop gaze. The website
renders these as layered 2D art with 3D-style lighting.
