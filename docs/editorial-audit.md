# Editorial refresh audit

## Mobile story scroll
The public build at `3b78417` rendered a 1,997 px story track and a 673 px sticky stage on a Pixel 7 browser profile, leaving approximately 1,324 px of pinned travel. Computed body overflow was visible and no dialog was open. The pinned view and scroll-driven canvas frame loads were concrete problems in the reported area. A permanent scroll lock on physical Safari or Android hardware was not isolated. The refreshed story uses normal document flow, a small photo-only parallax, and explicit story and chapter controls. It no longer creates or decodes a frame sequence.
